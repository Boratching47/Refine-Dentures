Questions

Good to know.

How much do dentures cost?

It depends on the type of denture and materials. Your first consultation is free and you'll get a fixed written quote before anything starts. Payment plans are available.

How long does the process take?

Most new dentures take [3 to 5] appointments over a few weeks. Repairs can often be done the same day.

Do I need a referral from my dentist?

No. You can book with us directly. If you need treatment from a dentist first, we'll let you know.

Will my dentures look natural?

Yes. You choose the tooth shape and shade with us, and you see a try-in version before your dentures are finished.

What if they feel uncomfortable?

A short adjustment period is normal. Follow-up adjustments are included, so just call us and we'll fix any sore spots.
